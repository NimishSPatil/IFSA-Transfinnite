How "As of" works

Picking a week shows the network as the company could have seen it on the Monday after that week. Nothing later is used, so you can replay last summer week by week without hindsight.

  • Depot stock, sales and write-offs: the weekly depot report, received the Monday after.
  • Quick-commerce sell-through: daily, the next day.
  • Plant pulp: the opening stock of the week; production at the end of the week.
  • Pulp orders: shown when placed; "received" only once they arrive.
  • Highway closures: only once they have started.

The dashboard assumes depot reports reach Planning weekly. Today the case says Planning gets consolidated sales only at month-end; the Demand page shows that gap.
