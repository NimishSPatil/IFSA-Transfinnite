How the simulated feed was built

Seeded (2026) Poisson counts by day × flavor × region × tier, scaled by the season. Events are placed only where the case says something happened: Dragon Passion launch buzz (F51), the influencer reel two weeks later (F52), stock-out complaints for PP and DP during the May closure (F43, F59), rival-launch chatter (F62), and one synthetic false alarm (a Guava Chilli meme in February that never reaches sales) to test the verify rule. The hidden test data is never read.
